HOW IT WORKS

Two Ways to Join.

Whether you want to learn single skills as your schedule allows, or enjoy daily open studio access with monthly class credits — we have a path for you.

OPTION 01 · PAY AS YOU GO

Single Sessions.

No long-term commitment. Pick the topics that interest you and join hands-on 2-hour classes shaped directly by member availability.

Step 01 — Interest Form
Submit an interest form (Free)
Select topics & mark preferred times with zero commitment
→
Step 02 — Instant Enrollment
Buy a Single Class ($45–$65)
Browse Current Curriculum & reserve your spot
→
Step 03 — We Handle the Rest
Class Scheduling & Lock-In
Once a class reaches 10–12 members, we lock in the date, confirm your spot, and send full preparation details.
OPTION 02 · UNLIMITED ACCESS

Studio Membership.

Get direct access to our physical space for independent projects, open studio time, and creative pursuits like painting, knitting, or meditating in the garden.
