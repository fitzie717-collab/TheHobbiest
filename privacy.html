Privacy Policy.

Last updated May 13, 2026

The Hobbiest Skills Club ("The Hobbiest," "we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why, and what choices you have. It applies to thehobbiestskillsclub.com and to the in-person services we provide at 135 N Main Street, Pittston, PA.

Information we collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you give us — name, email, phone number, mailing address, payment information when you sign up for a membership or class, plus optional details like your interests and availability when you fill out a member form.
  • Information from your visit — pages viewed, links clicked, device and browser type, approximate location, and time spent on the site. Collected through standard web analytics.
  • Information from class participation — attendance, class history, and any photos taken of the space (we'll tell you when photography is happening and let you opt out).

How we use it

  • To run classes, memberships, and events you've signed up for.
  • To send you the updates you've asked for — class schedules, member news, and the Founder's List newsletter.
  • To improve the experience, including which classes to offer and how the schedule is built.
  • To meet legal, safety, and accounting obligations.

We never sell your personal information.

Who we share with

We share information only with service providers who help us run The Hobbiest, and only what they need to do their job. That includes:

  • Beehiiv — for sending newsletters and managing email subscriptions.
  • Payment processors — for handling class fees, membership dues, and offsite bookings.
  • Class instructors — they see the roster (name, contact info) of students enrolled in their classes.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — only if legally required.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies for the site to function (remembering preferences, keeping you signed in) and to understand which parts of the site are most useful. You can disable cookies in your browser, though some features may stop working if you do.

Your rights

You can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it or delete it.
  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link at the bottom of every message.
  • Opt out of having your photo used in our marketing.

Email us at pittston@thehobbiestskillsclub.com and we'll handle it within a reasonable timeframe (usually under 30 days).

Children

The Hobbiest is a club for adults. Our website and services are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If we find we have, we'll delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you're a member or contact, and for a reasonable period afterward to handle accounting, legal, and member-history reasons. Then we delete or anonymize it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are material, we'll let you know by email.
