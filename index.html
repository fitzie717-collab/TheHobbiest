Creative Pursuits

In the Space

Environment Curation.

To protect the focus of our members and the intentionality of our sessions, we maintain a quiet, distraction-free environment.

  • No personal electronics or WiFi.
  • Adults-only (18+) environment.
  • Tobacco and alcohol-free premise.
  • Quiet focus during instruction.
