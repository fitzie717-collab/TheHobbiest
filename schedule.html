The Summer Curriculum.

Twenty classes across the four cornerstones of The Hobbiest curriculum, beginning this July. Each runs as a focused four-session course — two hours per session — held in studio or outdoors in the studio's side yard. Classes open as they fill, so tell us what you want and we'll put it on the calendar. Programs combine related classes into curated arcs.
