Pittston, Pa. — May 2026

After more than a decade building novel data and AI systems — from the Executive Office at Goldman Sachs to a patent-pending marketing technology named a semi-finalist in the annual Harvard President's Innovation Challenge — Ashley Fitzgerald is turning her attention to a different kind of unsolved problem: helping communities rebalance life in the digital age.

This July, the Clarks Summit native and Abington Heights High School graduate will open The Hobbiest: Skills Club, a private skills club for adults in downtown Pittston. The Hobbiest is a response to the gap Fitzgerald noticed when friends from different backgrounds and locations were all complaining about the same things: the difficulty of meeting new people, developing healthier habits and routines, and feeling burnt out from being accessible 24/7 in the digital age.

"My background has uniquely positioned me to quickly recognize flaws in complex systems and build novel solutions to address them. Once I saw the pattern, I realized pretty quickly that I was in a position to use my skills to help optimize the offline, human experience instead of the online, digital ones I have solved for thus far."

— Ashley Fitzgerald, Founder

There is a deliberate irony in her pitch: Fitzgerald spends her days building artificial intelligence, yet the club she is opening asks members to leave their screens at the door. She sees no contradiction. The same understanding of how technology is designed to capture attention, she says, is exactly what convinced her that people need a place to go to take it back.

That philosophy shapes the space itself. The Hobbiest is intentionally free of electronics and WiFi, adults-only, and food- and alcohol-free, designed so members can be fully present with the experience in front of them. Classes are expert-led and immersive, organized around five curriculum cornerstones: Creative Pursuits, Presence & Entertaining, Essential Life Skills, Hearth & Garden, and Living Well — covering everything from watercolor and calligraphy, to home repair and hosting, to intentional daily practices, all under the motto "Lessons, for Life."

Lessons begin at $99 for a focused four-session course, two hours per session — roughly $12 an hour. Fitzgerald says that price point is itself an innovation.

"There is a significant amount of innovation and IP in how we created our business and operations model that allowed us to offer courses in a financially accessible way while creating a real, for-profit business aimed at driving maximum positive impact on the individual and community. The back-end innovations are only outshined by the front-end innovation of creating a new type of place that enriches every part of what it means to be human, not least of which are creativity, potential, and community."

— Ashley Fitzgerald, Founder

The Hobbiest plans to expand its offerings in 2027 with curated multi-class Skills Packages and Offsites — immersive experiences taught beyond the classroom.

The club is also the first piece of a larger plan for the neighborhood. Fitzgerald intends to follow it with additional ventures built around the same idea; for-profit ventures that focus on enriching the offline human experience.

"We're building this in Pittston because we believe more people are beginning to realize that the version of success we've been sold — one centered around big-city living and endless consumption — was better in marketing materials than it is in real life. As someone who's been to that mountaintop, I can say that hearing the crickets at night is better than hearing horns, knowing your neighbors is better than hustling past strangers, and building a life rooted in purpose and connection is better than chasing the next thing off the conveyor belt. The Hobbiest is where it starts. The bigger mission is to restore balance in the digital age — and to build businesses that elevate, rather than exploit, the daily human experience."

— Ashley Fitzgerald, Founder

The club opens in downtown Pittston in July 2026. Members can join, and prospective instructors can apply to teach, at thehobbiestskillsclub.com.

About

The Hobbiest

The Hobbiest is a private skills club for adults in downtown Pittston, Pennsylvania, founded on the belief that confidence comes from capability. Through expert-led, immersive, phone-free classes, members build dynamic skillsets, cultivate taste, and curate a life of their own design. Lessons, for life.

About

The Founder

Ashley Fitzgerald is a data scientist and the founder of DeliverAI Solutions, an AI and machine-learning company behind the patent-pending Content Mix Model™. A 2026 semi-finalist in the Harvard President's Innovation Challenge, her career spans more than a decade in data and analytics — including time as an associate in the Executive Office at Goldman Sachs, where she led global analytics for brand marketing, plus senior analytics roles at Ad Results Media and Appnovation. A graduate of Abington Heights High School in Clarks Summit, she is completing a Master of Liberal Arts in Data Science from Harvard Extension School and holds a B.A. in Economics from Ithaca College.
