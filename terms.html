Who can join

The Hobbiest is a club for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to sign up for a membership or class on your own. We can't admit minors except in specifically marked family or guardian classes.

Membership

Memberships are billed monthly. Founder pricing — locked in for as long as your membership remains continuously active — is offered to members who join during the founding window (September 2026 onward) and stays at that rate until you cancel.

You can cancel anytime; we don't lock you in. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle. We don't prorate or refund partial months.

Classes & registration

To take a class you'll need to register and pay the class fee. Class spots are first-come, first-served unless a class is designated members-only.

Cancellations & refunds

  • More than 7 days before class: full refund or credit toward another class.
  • 2–7 days before class: 50% refund or full class credit.
  • Less than 48 hours: no refund, but you can transfer your spot to someone else by emailing us.
  • If we cancel a class (instructor illness, weather, etc.): you get a full refund or credit, your choice.

Materials and supplies

Some classes include materials in the fee; others ask you to bring your own. The class listing always says which. If a class has a materials fee on top of the class fee, that's listed too.

Life Bundles & Offsites

Life Bundles are sold as a package; the bundle fee secures your spot in all the classes in the bundle. If you can't attend a session in your bundle, we'll do our best to substitute another class of equal value.

Offsite classes happen at locations outside our Main Street space (gardens, garages, parks, etc.). You're responsible for getting yourself to and from the offsite location. Specific risks of the activity (you're outdoors, you're handling tools, etc.) will be described in the class listing.

Code of conduct

The Hobbiest works because it's a thoughtful, focused space. We expect every member, guest, and instructor to:

  • Be 18 or older. The Hobbiest is an adults-only space.
  • Keep your phone away. The Hobbiest is a cell-phone-free space and we don't offer WiFi. The whole point is to spend an hour or two without a screen.
  • Treat others with kindness and respect — regardless of skill level, background, or identity.
  • Show up on time and present for the class you signed up for.
  • Leave the space cleaner than you found it.
  • No alcohol in the space; no smoking inside the building; no outside food. We provide water, tea, and coffee — save your meals for before or after class.
  • Follow safety instructions from instructors, especially in classes with tools, heat, sharp objects, or outdoor settings.
  • Hold photos until the end. Your instructor will invite a brief photo moment in the final minutes of class. Before then, please keep cameras and phones put away.

We reserve the right to refuse service to, or end the membership of, anyone whose behavior is harassing, dangerous, or repeatedly disruptive. We don't refund the unused portion of a membership ended for cause.

Photography & sharing

The Hobbiest is a phone-free space. To preserve the focus of class, we ask that you keep your camera and phone away during the session. In the last few minutes, your instructor will invite a brief moment to photograph your work, the class, or both — and we'd love for you to share what you made on Instagram, with friends, anywhere. Tag us if you like.

The Hobbiest itself occasionally photographs classes and events for our own marketing. When we plan to photograph, we'll let you know at the start of class and you can opt out. If you'd prefer never to be photographed in our space, tell us in writing and we'll honor that across every class you take.
