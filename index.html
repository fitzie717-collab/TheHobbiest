In the Space

A few quiet expectations.

Phones away. No WiFi. No alcohol. No outside food. No smoking. Adults only (18+).

Water, tea, and coffee are on us. Photos are held until the last minute of class — your instructor will invite a moment to capture your work, and we encourage you to share what you've made out in the world.
