Waiver & Policies

The Hobbiest Skills Club LLC · Pittston, Pennsylvania

Please read these policies and the waiver below. By checking the agreement box and entering your name at checkout, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to all of the following.

Enrolment & commitment

  • Each course is a four-week commitment, meeting weekly at its scheduled day and time.
  • Your spot is reserved when you enrol and confirmed when payment is complete.
  • Class sizes are limited; enrolment is first-come, first-served up to each course's capacity.

Refunds, cancellations & swaps

  • All sales are final. There are no refunds for missed classes.
  • If we cancel a class, it will be rescheduled or refunded for that session.
  • Exchanges or class swaps are based on availability.
  • Where a refund is issued at our discretion, it is reduced by the payment processing fee, which our payment processor does not return to us when a payment is refunded.

Eligibility

The Club is for adults 18 and older. Minors are not permitted in classes or on the premises.

House rules & conduct

The Club is an unplugged, substance-free space designed for focus and craft. By enrolling you agree to the following:

  • No alcohol, food, marijuana, or drugs on the premises.
  • No smoking or vaping.
  • Anyone who appears impaired will be asked to leave.
  • No cell phones, electronics, or wifi during class — being present is part of the experience.
  • No yelling or disruptive behavior. Please treat instructors, fellow members, and the space with respect.

We operate a one-strike policy. Disruptive behavior, impairment, or breaking these rules may result in immediate removal from class without a refund.
